PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: October 2, 2026

NumGuru (https://numguru.online/) collects information needed to create numerology reports, manage purchases, restore paid access, and respond to support requests.

For a free sample, we store the submitted date of birth and submission time. For a premium request, we store the name and date of birth entered in the form. At checkout, we store the name, email address, phone number, date of birth, order ID, payment ID, amount, payment status, and any promo code used.

Razorpay processes card and bank details. NumGuru does not store card numbers or bank credentials. Customer, form, and transaction records are stored in a restricted database. We do not sell personal data or send promotional emails without a separate subscription.

We use this information to generate reports, verify payments, provide or restore paid report access, maintain transaction records, and resolve support issues. A paid report access link is a bearer link; anyone with that link can view the associated report, so keep it private.

To ask what data we hold about you or request deletion, contact connectnumguru@gmail.com. We may retain transaction records where required for accounting or dispute resolution.
